# AutoMall vehicle-brand logo sources

Captured and checked 29 October 2026. All 26 catalogue brands have genuine identifying artwork. No logos were generated, redrawn or replaced with generic initials. Original colors and letterforms are preserved.

The 17 dataset images come from [car-logos-dataset](https://github.com/filippofilip95/car-logos-dataset), which records its upstream artwork URLs. Nine other marks come from the brand websites listed below. PNG work is limited to transparent-margin trimming, resizing and encoding. The SERES mark is the exact glyph rendered from its official website icon font.

White/inverse marks use a charcoal badge: AVATR, BYD Yangwang, GAC Aion, Geely Farizon and Rising. Other marks use an ivory badge, preserving legibility without recoloring the logos. Existing site trademark and independence notices apply.

## Validation

- All 26 brands in `catalog-data.json` map to a local asset through `brandLogoFor` in `app/src/brand-logos.ts`.
- All raster files decode successfully. All SVGs were parsed as XML; no scripts, event attributes, external references, embedded images, foreign objects or animation elements were present.
- All assets were visually inspected in a 26-brand contact sheet. Known brand shapes, spelling, contrast and transparent margins were checked.
- Total deployed logo bytes: 295781.
- Self-hosted files are used by the site; no logo API key, third-party runtime request or tracking embed is required.
